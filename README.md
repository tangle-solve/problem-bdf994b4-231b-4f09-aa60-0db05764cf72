### ALGO-000191: Quantum PCP conjecture for local Hamiltonians
- **Subfield:** Quantum complexity and locally decodable codes
- **Open:** Prove QMA-hardness of approximating ground-state energy to a fixed positive fraction of the number of bounded-norm local terms.
- **Status:** Open since the 2000s. Results on nonlocal games, NLTS, and good quantum LDPC codes do not by themselves resolve this local-Hamiltonian formulation.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would show that even coarse estimates of many-body ground energy can be quantum-computationally intractable.
- **Key works:** Aharonov, Arad, and Vidick (2013), *The Quantum PCP Conjecture*, arXiv:1309.7495, DOI: 10.1145/2491533.2491549.
- **Related:** Condensed-matter physics—complexity of low-energy states.
